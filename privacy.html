Data Lake Archive privacy policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

Data Lake Archive is a personal tool with one user, its developer. It backs up the developer's own market-data files to the developer's own Google Drive. It refuses to store an authorization from any other Google account.

What it accesses

The app uses Google's drive.file permission, so it can reach only the Drive files it created. It creates, reads, checks and removes those files. It also reads the account's email address, to confirm the right account authorized it, and the account's Drive storage usage. It reads nothing else in the account.

Where the data goes

The authorization and the account's email address are kept on the developer's own computer. The app sends Google user data only to Google. It has no server, analytics or advertising, and it does not sell or share Google user data.

How to remove it

The files stay in Google Drive until they are removed. Access can be withdrawn at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and the files can be deleted from Google Drive.

Contact
